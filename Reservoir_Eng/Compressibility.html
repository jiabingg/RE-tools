Equation used

Units are psi⁻¹. The inputs are displayed as ×10⁻⁶ psi⁻¹ for convenience; the final value is shown both as ×10⁻⁶ psi⁻¹ and scientific notation.

Hall rock-compressibility source

Hall defines the reported rock compressibility as change in pore volume per unit pore volume per psi. Figure 2 in the paper shows effective reservoir rock compressibility versus porosity for limestone and sandstone.

Figure 2 from Hall, H. N. 1953. “Compressibility of Reservoir Rocks,” SPE-953309-G.

Rock correlation implemented

cf = 1.86 × 10⁻⁶ × φ−0.415, where φ is porosity as a fraction

This is a practical curve-fit representation of Hall Figure 2. It is not recommended for unconsolidated sands or highly fractured formations.
