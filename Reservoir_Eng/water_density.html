For low salinity water, wt% is approximately TDS mg/L ÷ 10,000. This tool refines that slightly by iterating with the calculated brine density.

Density curve

Curve is a digitized/approximated version of McCain Fig. 16-8: “Effect of salinity on the density of brine.” Use for screening-level calculations, not as a substitute for measured density.

Reference points used by this tool

TDS, wt%Density, lb/ft³SG

Method

wt% ≈ TDS(mg/L) ÷ [10,000 × brine density(g/mL)]
SG = brine density(lb/ft³) ÷ fresh water density(lb/ft³)
Hydrostatic gradient = 0.433 × SG psi/ft
Pressure difference = Hydrostatic gradient × TVD difference

Suggested citation: McCain, W.D. Jr., The Properties of Petroleum Fluids, 2nd ed., PennWell, Chapter 16, Fig. 16-8, “Effect of salinity on the density of brine.” The figure cites data from International Critical Tables II, 1927, p. 79.
