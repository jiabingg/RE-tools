WBD Marker QC and Repair - Version 2.5

- Fixed automatic repair for horizontal and highly deviated wells with
  non-monotonic TVD survey values.
- The previous TVD interpolation assumed TVD always increased with page Y.
  For FREEMAN VH-1 (API 0403055510), TVD reaches about 1,797 ft and then
  decreases to about 1,714.5 ft along the horizontal section. The old engine
  truncated the usable TVD scale at 1,714.5 ft and rejected the 1,787.0 ft and
  1,787.6 ft marker picks.
- The engine now detects a non-monotonic TVD column and automatically uses the
  authoritative MD survey row to disambiguate page placement.
- A warning is written to the Activity log and repair summary whenever this
  fallback is used.
- Added Part125 / API 0403055510 to the smoke-test sample set.
